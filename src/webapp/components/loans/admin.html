Loan Requests

Both guarantors must consent first; then two different admins approve, and the second approval issues the loan. Member loans are capped at 50% of Principal savings (approving admins can override with a reason). Neither approver may be the initiator or the applicant. Reject requires a reason. A loan with security first needs its savings-coverage waiver approved by three committee administrators (Finance Committee and Chairperson); only then are the guarantors asked.
